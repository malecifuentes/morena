# Multi-Bakery Subscription Platform

## Overview

This project is a subscription-based digital platform designed for small bakeries and dessert businesses.

Instead of serving only one bakery, the platform allows multiple bakeries (tenants) to manage their own products, prices and branding independently. Morena, a Guatemalan bakery specializing in cakes, cupcakes, banana bread and desserts, is the pilot bakery used to validate the platform.

## Problem

Many small bakeries receive orders through WhatsApp or social media. Customers often need to exchange multiple messages just to ask about products, prices and availability, making the ordering process slow and inefficient.

## Solution

The platform allows each bakery to:

- Display its own product catalogue.
- Show current product prices.
- Receive online order requests.
- Keep its products and customer requests separated from other bakeries through tenant identification.
