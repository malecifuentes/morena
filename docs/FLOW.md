# Morena Bakery — Navigation Flow

| | Product Catalogue (home) | Product Details and Pricing |
|---|---|---|
| **Purpose** | Show Morena Bakery products at a glance and help customers choose what they want to explore. | Give customers the information they need to understand a selected product before continuing with an order request. |
| **Information shown** | Product name, category, price and availability. | Product name, description, price, availability and quantity options. |
| **Actions** | Browse the catalogue and open a product to see more details. | Review the product information, choose a quantity and return to the catalogue. |
| **Navigation** | Comes first when Morena Bakery opens. Opening a product leads to Product Details and Pricing. | Reached by opening a product from the catalogue. Back returns to Product Catalogue. |
| **Empty / error state** | "No products are available right now. Please check again soon." | "This product is not available right now. Please choose another product." |
