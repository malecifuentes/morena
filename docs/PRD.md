# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## Project

Multi-Bakery Subscription Platform (Morena Pilot)

## Overview

This project is a multi-tenant digital platform that allows different bakeries to manage their own online catalog, branding, and customer orders.

Morena is the pilot bakery used to validate the platform before onboarding additional bakeries.

## Problem

Many small bakeries receive orders manually through WhatsApp or social media, making it difficult to organize products, pricing, and customer requests efficiently.

## Target Users

- Bakery owners
- Bakery customers

## Goal

Provide each bakery with its own customizable online storefront where customers can browse products, view prices, and submit an online order request.

## MVP Features (Must)

- Product catalogue
- Product prices
- Online order request
## User Stories

- As a customer, I want to browse the products offered by a bakery so that I can decide what to order.
- As a customer, I want to see product prices before placing an order.
- As a customer, I want to submit an online order request quickly and easily.
- As a bakery owner, I want to manage my own catalogue without affecting other bakeries.
- As a bakery owner, I want my products and customer requests to remain separated from those of other bakeries.

## Out of Scope

The initial MVP does not include:

- Online payments
- Customer accounts and login
- Inventory management
- Delivery tracking
- Administrative dashboards
- Chatbot integration
- Third-party delivery services

## Success Criteria

The MVP will be considered successful when:

- Customers can view the catalogue of a selected bakery.
- Customers can view product prices.
- Customers can submit an online order request successfully.
- Each bakery only displays its own products and branding.
- Customer requests are stored under the correct bakery (tenant).
- Required fields are validated before an order request is submitted.
## Future Features

- Product customization
- Calendar availability
- Automatic quotations
- Delivery options
- Shopping cart
- Image upload
